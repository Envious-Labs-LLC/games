Tool: OpenAI built-in image generation

Mode: Identity-preserving edits of `source/characters/hanuman-wind.png`

All six edits preserved the same respectful Hanuman identity, face, crown,
jewelry, saffron and deep-red clothing, body proportions, golden gada design,
painterly fantasy rendering, lighting, and side-view camera angle. Each used a
flat `#ff00ff` chroma-key background with one character, two arms, two legs, no
cropping, text, logo, watermark, cast shadow, or magenta spill.

## Run A

Dynamic right-facing run. Right leg extended forward for heel contact, left leg
pushing back, natural counter-swing, slight forward lean, tail streaming back,
and gada carried low behind.

## Run B

Opposite phase of the right-facing run. Left leg extended forward for heel
contact, right leg pushing back, opposite arm counter-swing, slight forward
lean, tail streaming back, and gada carried low behind.

## Run Mid

Passing phase between the two long strides. One foot is planted beneath the
hips, the opposite knee is lifted clearly forward, the torso leans into the
run, and the gada stays carried behind. This frame makes the foot cycle legible
between the two contact poses.

## Air

Powerful right-facing airborne leap. Both feet off the ground, front knee
raised, rear leg extended, free arm reaching forward, torso angled into the
leap, tail streaming back, and gada carried behind and upward.

## Attack windup

Wide planted stance with bent knees and torso twisted back. Both hands grip the
single gada, raised high behind and above the head for a forward overhead swing.

## Attack impact

Wide planted impact stance with front knee bent and torso rotated forward. Both
hands grip the single gada, now extended low and far in front to the right after
traveling through a clear overhead arc.
